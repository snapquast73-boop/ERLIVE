Unofficial fan project. This page reads the public MLB StatsAPI from your browser. A field-error row is included only when a play's result event type is field_error; this is not a complete inventory of every error notation. A scorer-pending row is included only for the primary os_ruling_pending_primary event, not the prior/base-running marker. See the official MLB event-type registry. Every displayed event links to its game's official feed. Recent rows are saved in this browser only; cached rows not reconfirmed by the latest game-feed response are labeled and excluded from current counts. Sound and desktop notifications are optional browser features. Notifications are limited to active reviews with scoring movements associated in the feed; they do not predict a ruling or a score change. This build does not track historical scoring reclassifications. See Flagged for Review for verification scope and known limitations. Not affiliated with MLB or MLB Advanced Media.
